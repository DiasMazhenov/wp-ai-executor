# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Site owners, designers, and content teams using the WP AI Executor chat inside
Elementor. They describe the content in natural language and expect a complete,
editable, responsive block that can be reviewed and refined in the editor.

## Product Purpose

Generate useful Elementor page sections from content-only briefs while
preserving a coherent visual composition. Success means the rendered block
matches the requested content, has intentional hierarchy and spacing, uses
native Flexbox Containers, remains editable, and survives visual validation in
the real Elementor editor.

## Brand Personality

Intentional, editorial, confident. The experience should feel like a careful
design partner rather than a random layout generator.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI landing pages: repeated equal cards, detached badges or icons,
oversized headings with empty space, placeholder media, low-contrast text,
incompatible colors, clipped buttons, arbitrary widths, nested cards, and
layouts that do not match the supplied content or reference composition.

## Design Principles

- Content determines the composition; do not force a widget type or template
  when the brief does not require it.
- Use native Elementor Flexbox Containers with clear parent-child alignment,
  responsive sizing, and intentional gaps.
- Keep hierarchy, badges, icons, media, and calls to action visually related.
- Prefer varied, purposeful compositions over repeated card-grid scaffolding.
- Validate every live generation with a screenshot, Vision/design review,
  content comparison, and a corrective pass when the result fails.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, visible and correctly sized controls, meaningful image
alt text, responsive text that does not overflow, and layouts that remain usable
on mobile. Avoid relying on color alone to communicate meaning.
